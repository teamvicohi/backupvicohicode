How to Use

  1. Upload your Excel or CSV file using the file upload option. The file will be uploaded automatically.
  2. Paste your records into the Paste Raw Data box. Enter the data that you believe exists in the uploaded file.
  3. Click the 🔍 Analyze Data button to start the matching process.
  4. All matching records will be displayed in the results table below, along with their corresponding column details from the uploaded file.
  5. Use the available actions to manage your results:
    • 📋 Copy Data ▼ – Copy the complete results or copy data column-wise based on your requirements.
    • 📤 Export ▼ – Download the matched records directly in your preferred format.

⚠️ Recommendation

For large datasets and the best possible performance, we recommend using CSV (.csv) files whenever possible.

CSV files are processed faster and provide a smoother experience when working with thousands of records.
