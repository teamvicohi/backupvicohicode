What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, mobile, tablet) when you visit a website. They help websites function properly and improve user experience.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to provide, improve, and secure our services, including:

  • Remembering user preferences and settings
  • Improving website performance and speed
  • Analyzing traffic and user behavior
  • Enhancing security and preventing abuse
  • Serving relevant advertisements

Types of Cookies We Use

1. Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. Without them, some services may not work.

2. Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the website so we can improve functionality and performance.

3. Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences such as language, region, or settings for a better experience.

4. Advertising Cookies

These cookies may be used by Google AdSense and advertising partners to display personalized ads based on your browsing behavior.
